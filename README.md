# NovaCare –Cybersecurity GRC Project

## About the Project

NovaCare is a simulated healthcare organisation used for this cybersecurity Governance and Compliance project.

Healthcare organisations handle sensitive patient information and depend on systems that must stay secure and available. Weak access controls, unpatched vulnerabilities, poor monitoring and gaps in backup protection can put both information and business operations at risk.

I created this case study to practise how cybersecurity risks can be identified, assessed, documented and managed in a practical business setting.

It include 8 assets assessed, 8 risks documented, 2 security exceptions, 17 ISO 27001 control checks, 17 remediation actions, 1 dashboard and 1 one-page management report.

## What I Worked On

### 1. Cybersecurity Risk Assessment
Identified and assessed cybersecurity risks affecting NovaCare's systems and information. Documented the risks, existing controls, likelihood, impact, and inherent and residual risk.

### 2. Risk Treatment Plan
Recorded recommended actions to reduce each risk, assigned owners, and tracked priorities and target dates.

### 3. Security Exception Management
Documented two security exceptions, including the reason, the linked risk, compensating controls, approval status and review date.

### 4. ISO 27001 Control Gap Assessment and Remediation Plan
Reviewed selected ISO/IEC 27001:2022 controls linked to each risk, recorded the gaps and their severity, and turned them into remediation actions with owners, target dates and evidence requirements.

### 5. Risk Dashboard and Management Report
Organised the results into a dashboard and a one-page management report that highlights key risks, open actions and decisions needed from management.

## Key Scenario

One of the main risks involved five privileged service accounts that were exempt from multi-factor authentication (MFA), had broad administrative privileges and had no network restrictions. Monitoring alerts were generated but not routinely reviewed.

I rated this risk High before and after existing controls. I recommended that the exception should not be approved until the compensating controls (restricting network access, reducing privileges and assigning a named alert reviewer) are in place and checked.

## Tools and Frameworks

- Microsoft Excel: risk register, treatment plan, exception register, gap assessment, remediation plan and dashboard
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022: selected Annex A controls
- Risk assessment and treatment principles: identifying risks, evaluating existing controls and planning corrective actions

## Project Scope

This project focuses on identifying security gaps and documenting treatment actions. It does not claim that the remediation has been carried out or that control effectiveness has been independently checked.
I assessed a selection of ISO 27001 controls, not the whole standard. I have not mapped the results to NIS2, NIST CSF or CyFun
NovaCare is a simulated case study. The system details are my own assumptions.
